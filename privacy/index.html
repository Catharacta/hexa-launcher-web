Privacy Policy for Hexa Launcher

Last Updated: February 2026

1. Data Collection

Hexa Launcher is a local application. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. All your settings, shortcuts, and configurations are stored securely and locally on your Windows device.

2. Network Communication

The application does not phone home, use telemetry, or require an internet connection to function for its core features. Network connectivity is only required for features that inherently depend on it, such as downloading updates or communicating with the Microsoft Store for license verification.

3. Microsoft Store Integration

When purchased or installed through the Microsoft Store, Microsoft collects standard analytical data (such as installation counts and crash reports) in accordance with the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Hexa Launcher uses the Windows SDK to verify licenses, and this process is managed by your operating system.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not integrate third-party analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising frameworks into Hexa Launcher or this website.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us via our Support Page or the issue tracker on our official GitHub repository.
